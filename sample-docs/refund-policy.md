# Acme Gadgets: Refund & Support Policy

Last updated: July 2026

## Refund window

- Phones and laptops: 7 days from delivery for change-of-mind returns, provided the device is unused and all seals are intact.
- Accessories (cases, chargers, earbuds, cables): 14 days from delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- Open-box clearance items: final sale, no change-of-mind refunds.

## Damaged on arrival

If an item arrives damaged, contact support within 48 hours with unboxing photos. We will replace the item or refund the full purchase price, including shipping.

## Warranty

- Standard manufacturer warranty applies to all branded devices.
- Acme Care extended warranty can be added at checkout for 12 or 24 months.
- Water damage and unauthorized repairs void warranty coverage.

## Support hours

Support is available Monday-Saturday, 9:00-18:00 Asia/Manila.

Email: support@acme-gadgets.example
Chat: in-app Help Desk

## Shipping

Standard Metro Manila delivery is 1-2 business days.
Provincial delivery is 3-5 business days.
Express same-day is available for selected cities and carts above PHP 3,000.
